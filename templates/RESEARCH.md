# Research
---

*Copy this template to `agent-docs/research/README.md`; its links resolve from there. Keep the rules below unless the project differs. If you change the report location or use another guide for accepted decisions, update the paths and pointers together. Remove this editing instruction from the finished guide.*

Save requested research reports beside this guide at `agent-docs/research/<SLUG>.md`, relative to the project root. Use descriptive filenames for their topics.

Before investigating a topic or making a decision that prior research may inform, list the reports here and read those relevant to the task. If no relevant report exists, proceed with the requested work.

Reports hold findings and supporting sources. A report's recommendation is not an approved decision. Use [design decisions](../design-decisions/README.md) for accepted choices and their rationale, linking to the relevant research under that guide's record and approval rules.
